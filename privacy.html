Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use Binance Invest, why it is used and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as email address, username, name and optional phone number.
  • Authentication, session and security information needed to maintain account access.
  • Deposit, withdrawal, investment, referral and support records submitted through the platform.
  • Technical information such as device type, browser, approximate network information and service logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide and secure accounts, process and display requests, maintain transaction and referral records, respond to support tickets, prevent abuse, improve functionality and comply with applicable obligations.

3. Storage and service providers

Authorized technology providers may process limited information to deliver authentication, database, hosting, email, security or payment-related functionality. They receive only information reasonably required for their role and are subject to their own privacy and security terms.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to authorized providers, when required by applicable law, to protect users or the platform, or as part of a legitimate business restructuring subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention and security

Records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, transaction history, dispute handling, security and legal obligations. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information. Certain financial, security or legal records may need to be retained. You may also control browser storage through your device settings, although this can affect login and language preferences.

7. Contact and updates

For a privacy question or request, contact support@binancinvest.com. We may update this policy as the platform or applicable requirements change; the current effective date will be shown above.
