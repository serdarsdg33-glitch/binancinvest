Our Story & How We Pay Returns

Many users ask us what Binance Invest is and how payouts are generated. Founded in 2023, our platform was established to simplify financial growth and make global investment opportunities accessible to everyone without requiring expert trading skills.

Revenue Generation & Profit Distribution: Unlike traditional platforms that rely solely on transaction fees, our business model is backed by active deployment in real financial markets. User funds and platform capital are optimized across multiple streams:

  • Global Stock & Equity Markets: Investing in high-performing international corporate stocks and financial securities.
  • Digital Assets & Liquidity Pools: Participating in secure, high-yield liquidity provisioning and vetted digital asset opportunities.
  • Fee Management & Reserves: A portion of platform transaction fees is reinvested into user payout reserve funds to maintain stable returns.

This diversified approach aims to support distributions to users, although investment markets are volatile and returns are not guaranteed.
